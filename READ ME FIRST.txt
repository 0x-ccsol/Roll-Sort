ROLL SORT
Sorts one big folder of photos (say, a product shoot) into folders, by
clicking them. It also comes with Roll Merge, which puts film scans from two
(or more) cameras back into the order they were shot.

Both save copies in a new folder at the end. Your original files are never
changed.


FIRST TIME ONLY
1. Install Python 3 if the computer doesn't have it:
   https://www.python.org/downloads/
   (Windows: tick "Add python.exe to PATH" in the installer.)
2. Mac only: the first time, macOS may say the Start file "can't be opened".
   Right-click "Start Roll Sort (Mac).command" and choose Open, then Open again.
   On newer macOS: System Settings > Privacy & Security > scroll down > "Open Anyway".
3. The first start installs a couple of small parts (needs internet, ~1 minute).


EVERY TIME
1. Double-click "Start Roll Sort (Mac).command" (or the Windows one).
   A small window opens, and the app opens in your web browser.
   Keep that small window open while you work.
2. The first time, it asks what you'd like to do: Roll Sort (photos into
   folders) or Roll Merge (film rolls). It remembers your choice. To switch
   later, click the name at the top left. The two are kept completely
   separate: each has its own folders list, progress and settings.


ROLL SORT: SORTING A SHOOT INTO FOLDERS
1. Choose the folder with the photos.
2. Add a folder for each group with "+ Folder" (e.g. Apples, Oranges).
3. Click a photo to put it in the current folder ("Sort here" switches
   folder). Tips:
   - To do many at once: Shift-click the first photo, then Shift-click the
     last one. Everything in between is selected; press Enter to put them in
     the current folder. "Clear selection" (or Esc) starts again.
   - Sorted photos leave a blank space, so nothing moves while you click.
     Press R (or "Refresh board") to close up the gaps. It also does this by
     itself after 12 gaps, once you stop for a moment (or choose "off").
   - Space shows the photo under the mouse large. Click outside it to close.
   - Z undoes. Your progress saves by itself, so you can quit and carry on.
   - "Banish from sorting": tick it, then click bad shots or duplicates to
     leave them out. Nothing is deleted: "Banished" lets you get them back.
4. Review & export: check each folder. Drag a photo to move it, click a
   folder's name to rename it. Then Export. Each photo is copied into its
   folder, keeping its original name (or named "Apples 001" ...) and its own
   date.


ROLL MERGE: FILM ROLLS FROM TWO CAMERAS
1. Choose the folder the lab sent (the one with a folder for each roll).
2. Sort the rolls under the camera that shot them, in shooting order.
   Scroll along a roll's photos with the mouse wheel (or the bar under them),
   and click one to see it large, if you're not sure.
3. Click whichever photo came next. Tips:
   - Left / right arrow keys pick camera 1 / camera 2.
   - Click a small photo further along to place a whole run at once.
   - Z undoes.
   - Folders: click "+ Pre-Ceremony · The-Ceremony · Reception", then
     "Sort here" on a folder to start filling it.
   - The mouse wheel scrolls the strip of placed photos at the top.
   - "Remove from timeline": tick it, then click photos in the top strip to
     send them back down to be placed again. Untick it (or press Esc) after.
   - "Banish from sorting": tick it, then click bad frames or exact duplicates
     to leave them out. Nothing is deleted: "Banished" lets you get them back.
   - Your progress saves by itself. You can quit and carry on later.
4. Review the full timeline. You can still drag photos to fix their order and
   click a folder's name to rename it. Undo, or "Revert all changes made on
   this screen", fixes mistakes. Then Export. The copies are numbered
   (0001, 0002 ...) and get capture times in order, so Lightroom sorts them
   correctly. To be safe, sort by File Name.


TRY IT FIRST
- "Fruit demo" (for Roll Sort): 30 mixed-up photos of apples, oranges and
  bananas. Add folders like Apples, Oranges and Bananas and sort them. Each
  photo says what's in it, so you can check the result.
- "Demo wedding" (for Roll Merge): 4 short practice rolls from 2 cameras.
  Each photo shows the time it was "taken". Camera 1 shot the rolls ending
  2 and 4, camera 2 shot 1 and 3.


GOOD TO KNOW
- Works with JPG and TIFF.
- Preview pictures are kept so photos show quickly. By default they're
  cleared after a successful export (untick that if you'll come back to the
  same photos). You can also clear them from the first screen.
- "Start completely fresh" on the first screen forgets everything saved.
  Your photos and exported copies are never touched.
- Quit with the Quit button in the app, or by closing the small window.
